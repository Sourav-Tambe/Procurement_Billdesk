Invoice details: header card, every scenario

One template, drawn below for each state. The layout is the compliance export permit details (your Figma frame): a tinted box of label-over-value pairs in four columns, and the invoice document as a card on the right. The four info tiles in today's second card move into this box. Who is looking only changes the buttons at the top right.

How the card handles each case

  • Documents: the card shows the first file (the invoice). A PDF shows the PDF icon on the peach tile, and a photo shows its thumbnail. More files add "+n more" under the card, which opens the viewer on the next one. With no file, a dashed box says "No invoice uploaded", and Upload shows only to the clerk while they hold the invoice.
  • The ⋮ on the card: View, Download, and Remove (the last only for the clerk while they hold it).
  • Challans: up to three links, then "+n more", which opens the challan list. Before any are added: "None added yet".
  • Long text: the file name ends in "…" with the full name on hover. A long vendor name wraps in the title, and values wrap inside their column.
  • Messages: a returned or rejected invoice shows one band above the box with who, when and the note. Nothing else moves.
  • Buttons by role and stage: clerk (Pending match, Correction needed): action plus ⋮ Edit / Delete. Head (Under review): Send back plus Approve, or Accept as billed and Pay as received on discrepancies. Anything else: no buttons.
  • Narrow windows: the columns and the card wrap. Nothing overlaps.
