Bill Desk with GRNs

n challans go into 1 GRN, and 1 GRN is billed by 1 or n invoices. The example below uses real challans from the Diet & Kitchen snapshot. Every invoice step after saving stays as it is today.

What's decided

Your answers from this session.

The clerk makes the GRNOn Challans, the clerk ticks challans and clicks Make GRN.
One vendor per GRNA selection with two vendors is refused.
Whole item linesEach GRN item line goes on exactly one invoice.
Matched per invoiceEach invoice goes to the head on save, as today.
