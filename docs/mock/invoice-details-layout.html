Invoice details: rail on top, attachments and activity on the right

Layout. The stage rail moves into the header card, under the title and challans. The details card splits: on the left the info tiles, Items and Challans, as now; on the right a 360px column with Attachments on top and Activity below. Because every file now has room, the header no longer carries a document card.

How it holds up

  • Attachments: every file as a document card, two to a row (PDF icon, or a photo's thumbnail; name; ⋮ View / Download). Past four files: the first four and "+n more", which opens the viewer. The clerk holding the invoice gets "+ Add".
  • Activity: the invoice's trail, newest first: who (initials in their role's colour), what, when, and any note in a box (a send-back note in the warning tint). Past five events: "Show all (n)".
  • Rail: as built. Who moved each done step and when; the current step's state; a return or rejection in red.
  • Narrow windows: under 1200px the right column drops below the content, Attachments then Activity.
